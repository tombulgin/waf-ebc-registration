Why meet during World Aviation Festival?

World Aviation Festival brings the aviation industry together in Lisbon from 13-15 October 2026 at Feira Internacional de Lisboa, offering a timely opportunity for senior industry leaders to explore the future of airline retail, digital customer engagement, payments and operational transformation with Microsoft and recognised partners: Adyen and Amicis.

Revenue growth

Explore how airlines can expand ancillary and retail opportunities through more connected, payment-ready customer journeys.

Service transformation

Discuss customer service, contact centre efficiency and improved digital experiences across the traveller lifecycle.

Partner ecosystem

See how Microsoft, Adyen and Amicis capabilities can combine into a repeatable inflight retail solution play.
